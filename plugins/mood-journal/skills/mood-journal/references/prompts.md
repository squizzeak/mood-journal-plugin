# Optional reflection prompts

After required storage setup, or for explicitly requested unsaved reflection, offer a style when requested or when it fits the user's aim; do not prescribe a regimen or promise health outcomes. One prompt at a time is usually enough.

| Style | Possible invitation |
| --- | --- |
| Free writing or morning pages | What is taking up the most space in your mind? Write freely for as long as feels useful. |
| Gratitude | Is there something you appreciated today, and what made it matter? Skip this if gratitude feels forced. |
| Evening reflection | What from today would you like to understand or remember? |
| Emotional processing | What are you noticing emotionally and in your body, and what might you need? |
| Decisions | What matters most in this choice? Separate known facts, uncertain expectations, and values. |
| Weekly/monthly review | Which changes recur across the available dated entries? What helped, and what remains uncertain? |
| Dialogue or future-self writing | Would a conversation between two perspectives, or a letter to your future self, help you explore this? Label imagined content clearly. |
| Habit support | What amount of journaling would be workable? Missed days need no catch-up or shame. |

Do not force positivity, repeatedly propose an exercise the user disliked, assume a single root cause from a “why” sequence, or convert imagined future events into factual records. User-chosen pacing and purpose govern.
